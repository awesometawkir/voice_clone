Getting a clone that sounds like you

  • Quiet room, close mic. Background noise and echo are copied into the clone.
  • Speak the way you want the videos to sound. The clone copies energy and pace, not just tone.
  • One continuous take of 15 to 30 seconds, no music, no long pauses.
  • Same language as your videos gives the most natural accent.
  • Keep two or three voices (calm, energetic) and choose per video.

Before you upload to YouTube

  • In YouTube Studio, under Altered or synthetic content, answer Yes when a video uses a cloned voice. The label does not limit reach or monetisation; hiding it can.
  • Monetisation depends on the video being original and useful, not on how the voice was made. Write your own scripts; do not mass-produce near-identical videos.
  • Only clone your own voice or one you have written permission for.
  • Upload the .srt file with each video: captions help search and retention.
